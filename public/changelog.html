What’s new

Changes to KASA.Rx, newest first.

25 November 2026

  • KASA.Rx now lives at kasarx.co.uk.
  • Booked shifts go into Google Calendar from any page, not just Shifts. If Google needs you to sign in again, Home shows the changes waiting, with one tap to send them.

24 November 2026

  • A personal greeting on Home, using the name you’d like to be called (set it when you sign up, or in Settings).
  • A new Home dashboard: this month vs last month, a 12-month earnings chart, where your work comes from, and recent activity.
  • A new admin dashboard with account, activity, feedback and request statistics.
  • KASA.Rx is now the operating and financial management platform for pharmacy services.
  • “What to do next” on Home: work done but not invoiced (grouped by pharmacy and month, with one-tap invoices), payments overdue, invoices due this week and pharmacies that often pay late.
  • New Home figures: expected income this month, work not invoiced, payments overdue and your estimated tax reserve.
  • Insights: services delivered, revenue, outstanding invoices, average time to payment, revenue by service type and monthly revenue, for any tax year or the last 12 months.
  • Payment reminders get firmer as an invoice gets later, and unpaid invoices show when each pharmacy usually pays.
  • Tax page: one clear “put aside” figure, a simple “put aside N% of every payment” rule, and a bar showing where your locum income goes (what you keep, tax, NI, student loan and expenses). The full breakdown is one tap away.
  • Student loans: choose your plan (1, 2, 4, 5 or Postgraduate) and the estimate includes repayments on your locum profit, alongside any PAYE job. Thresholds from GOV.UK for 2026 to 2027.
  • Estimated take-home after expenses, tax, NI and student loan.
  • Sole trader or limited company? A side-by-side guide on the Tax page: what you’d keep each way on the same profit (2026 to 2027 rates from GOV.UK), plus IR35, paperwork and record-keeping to weigh up. Not financial advice.
  • Privacy: a clear data retention table (crash reports 90 days, sent emails 30 days, accountant requests 12 months after they’re passed on, feedback 2 years), applied automatically; guidance on keeping records for HMRC; and a reminder not to enter patient information.

23 November 2026

  • Talk to an accountant: register interest and KASA will match you with an accredited accountant (Tax page, tax tips and the account menu).
  • App lock now defaults to 5 minutes, and admins can switch admin on with Google without logging out.
  • New KASA.Rx logo, app icon and browser icon.

22 November 2026 (evening)

  • App lock: open KASA.Rx with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or device PIN (Settings → App lock).
  • Reminders: notifications for invoices that need a follow-up and the evening before each shift (Settings → Reminders). Your next shift also shows on Home.
  • Receipts: “Take photo of receipt” opens the camera; “Choose a photo” picks one you’ve already taken.
  • Deleting your account now checks it’s you first, so nothing is removed if the password is wrong.
  • Downloads on an installed iPhone app open the share sheet so you can save or send the file.

22 November 2026

  • Every delete now has an Undo button, and deleting a service asks first.
  • Install KASA.Rx on your phone as an app; it opens and logs work without signal.
  • Spreadsheet exports use a maintained library (ExcelJS).
  • Tax, National Insurance, mileage, Making Tax Digital and invoice sums are checked by automated tests against HMRC’s own examples before every update.
  • Tax pages show when the figures were last checked against GOV.UK.
  • Accessibility fixes (screen-reader labels, contrast), smaller download, and clearer bank-detail checks.
  • Deleting your account now also deletes your feedback, queued emails and crash reports.
  • Crash reports so problems can be fixed quickly (no tracker data is sent).

22 November 2026 (earlier)

  • Shifts calendar: click empty space to book, click a shift to change it; change or delete “this and later” weekly repeats.
  • Accounting: “Where to keep your savings”, with links to live rate tables and an interest estimator.

21 November 2026

  • Making Tax Digital: your four quarterly updates with HMRC’s deadlines, and an export for HMRC-recognised software.
  • Tax tips for locum pharmacists, each from GOV.UK, including PAYE plus locum work and where to claim your GPhC fee.
  • Compare claiming expenses with the trading allowance (£3,000 from April 2027).

20 November 2026

  • Shifts: book work on a calendar, sync with Google Calendar and add worked shifts to your log.
  • Accounting: budget split, other income and links to the Tax tab.
  • New Home page, privacy policy, KASA.Rx branding and a fresh design with dark and light modes.
  • Phone navigation bar, faster first load, and a short welcome for new accounts.
  • Emails sent through the admin are checked and limited, and admin requires Google sign-in.

19–20 November 2026

  • Journey mileage calculator with a map, expenses with receipt photos, tax estimate and PDF invoices.
  • Mileage service, pharmacy profiles and a smarter Invoices page.
  • KASA.Rx launched: accounts on any device, invoices by email, follow-ups and payment dates in your calendar.
