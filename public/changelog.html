What’s new

Changes to KASA.Rx, newest first.

22 November 2026 (evening)

  • App lock: open KASA.Rx with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or device PIN (Settings → App lock).
  • Reminders: notifications for invoices that need a follow-up and the evening before each shift (Settings → Reminders). Your next shift also shows on Home.
  • Receipts: “Take photo of receipt” opens the camera; “Choose a photo” picks one you’ve already taken.
  • Deleting your account now checks it’s you first, so nothing is removed if the password is wrong.
  • Downloads on an installed iPhone app open the share sheet so you can save or send the file.

22 November 2026

  • Every delete now has an Undo button, and deleting a service asks first.
  • Install KASA.Rx on your phone as an app; it opens and logs work without signal.
  • Spreadsheet exports use a maintained library (ExcelJS).
  • Tax, National Insurance, mileage, Making Tax Digital and invoice sums are checked by automated tests against HMRC’s own examples before every update.
  • Tax pages show when the figures were last checked against GOV.UK.
  • Accessibility fixes (screen-reader labels, contrast), smaller download, and clearer bank-detail checks.
  • Deleting your account now also deletes your feedback, queued emails and crash reports.
  • Crash reports so problems can be fixed quickly (no tracker data is sent).

22 November 2026 (earlier)

  • Shifts calendar: click empty space to book, click a shift to change it; change or delete “this and later” weekly repeats.
  • Accounting: “Where to keep your savings”, with links to live rate tables and an interest estimator.

21 November 2026

  • Making Tax Digital: your four quarterly updates with HMRC’s deadlines, and an export for HMRC-recognised software.
  • Tax tips for locum pharmacists, each from GOV.UK, including PAYE plus locum work and where to claim your GPhC fee.
  • Compare claiming expenses with the trading allowance (£3,000 from April 2027).

20 November 2026

  • Shifts: book work on a calendar, sync with Google Calendar and add worked shifts to your log.
  • Accounting: budget split, other income and links to the Tax tab.
  • New Home page, privacy policy, KASA.Rx branding and a fresh design with dark and light modes.
  • Phone navigation bar, faster first load, and a short welcome for new accounts.
  • Emails sent through the admin are checked and limited, and admin requires Google sign-in.

19–20 November 2026

  • Journey mileage calculator with a map, expenses with receipt photos, tax estimate and PDF invoices.
  • Mileage service, pharmacy profiles and a smarter Invoices page.
  • KASA.Rx launched: accounts on any device, invoices by email, follow-ups and payment dates in your calendar.
