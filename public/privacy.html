Privacy policy and disclaimer

In short: your entries, invoices and expenses belong to you. Only you can see them. We use them only to run the tracker for you, we never sell them, and you can download or delete everything at any time.

Not financial or tax advice

KASA.Rx is a record-keeping tool. Its tax, National Insurance, student loan, take-home and mileage figures, its comparison of working as a sole trader or through a limited company, and its suggestions, such as whether to claim your expenses or the trading allowance, are estimates and a general guide only. They are not financial, tax, accounting or legal advice, and they are not tailored to your circumstances.

The estimates use the rates and rules we understand to apply, including changes the government has announced but not yet made law, and they leave things out (for example pensions, savings interest, other investment income and payments on account). Rules change, and your situation may differ.

Check your figures with HMRC’s guidance or a qualified accountant before you file a tax return or make financial decisions. You are responsible for your own tax return and records. So far as the law allows, KASA is not liable for losses from relying on the tracker’s estimates.

Who we are

KASA.Rx, run by KASA, is the operating and financial management platform for pharmacy services. It helps UK locum pharmacists log their work, invoice pharmacies and keep track of payments, expenses and tax. In this policy, “we” and “KASA” mean the people who run KASA.Rx. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Contact us about privacy or anything else at KASA.Scripts@gmail.com.

Please don’t enter patient information

KASA.Rx is for your own business records. It isn’t designed to hold patient information, and it doesn’t need any. When you log a consultation or service, record the service, date, quantity and fee only: don’t add patient names, initials, dates of birth, NHS numbers or anything else that could identify a patient, in descriptions, notes, receipt photos or anywhere else. If you find you’ve added some, edit or delete it.

Information about other people

The pharmacies you save (names, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses) are business contact details you add to invoice them. They’re only visible to you and are deleted with your account. Please only add details you’re entitled to use for invoicing.

Who can see your information

  • Only you can see your entries, invoices, pharmacies, journeys, expenses and receipts. Security rules on our database stop anyone else, including us, from reading them through the tracker.
  • The site administrator can see your name, email address, and sign-up and last log-in dates, so they can help with account problems. They can also see feedback you send.
  • If you ask the tracker to send an invoice from the site’s email address instead of your own, that email, including the invoice PDF, is passed to the administrator to send for you.

Connecting your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. You choose what to connect in Settings, and the tracker asks Google for permission each time:

  • Gmail: only to send the invoice and reminder emails you choose to send. The tracker cannot read your inbox.
  • Google Calendar: to add the payment due dates and booked shifts you ask for, keep those shift events up to date (and remove them when you delete a shift), and show the titles and times of your other events beside your shifts, so you can turn one into a shift. Your other events are only shown on your screen and aren’t saved, unless you turn one into a shift.
  • Google Sheets (administrator only): to keep a sheet of account holders and feedback in the administrator’s own Google Drive. Only files the tracker creates can be reached.

Google access is kept in your browser for the current visit only and is not stored on our servers. KASA.Rx’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings, or remove access from your Google account permissions.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores your account and tracker data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • When you work out a journey, the places you type are sent to postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find them, and to OSRM to measure the driving route. Map images come from OpenStreetMap. These services do not receive your name or account details.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

Cookies and storage on your device

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t run analytics. Your browser keeps a few things so the tracker works: your log-in (if you chose “Keep me logged in”), an offline copy of your tracker and of the site itself (so it loads quickly, can be installed as an app and works without signal), your light or dark mode choice, and which Google services you’ve connected. If you turn them on: reminder settings and the list of upcoming reminders (kept on the device to show notifications), and the app lock. The app lock uses your device’s own Face ID, fingerprint or PIN check: your fingerprint or face never leaves your device and we never receive it; we only keep a random key ID on the device.

Our legal basis

  • Running the tracker for you (your account, tracker, invoices, tax estimates and emails you ask us to send): performance of a contract with you.
  • Feedback, account support, crash reports and keeping the service secure: our legitimate interest in running and improving the tracker safely.
  • Sharing a “Talk to an accountant” request with an accountant, and connecting Google: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We don’t make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. The tax figures are estimates shown to you; nothing is decided for you.

KASA.Rx is for UK pharmacists and isn’t meant for anyone under 18.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, delete or download the information we hold about you, and object to or restrict how we use it. You can do most of this yourself:

  • Change your details in Settings
  • Download your data in Settings → Your data (Excel or a back-up file)
  • Delete everything in Settings → Delete account

For anything else, email KASA.Scripts@gmail.com or use Send feedback in the tracker. We’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Keeping your information safe

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts it at rest. Database security rules let only you read your tracker. Administrator access needs a Google sign-in, so it’s protected by Google’s 2-Step Verification, and deleting an account needs you to confirm who you are first. You can also turn on an app lock that uses your device’s Face ID, fingerprint or PIN.

No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password that you don’t use elsewhere, and lock your phone. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we’ll tell you without undue delay and report it to the Information Commissioner’s Office where the law requires.
